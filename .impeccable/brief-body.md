## Direction contract

THESIS: The printed reading-plan sheet you keep in your Bible, rendered as an e-paper screen. It refuses the category's card stack, streak flames and photo heroes.
OWN-WORLD: E-paper grey ground, near-black ink, flat 1–2px solid rules, no shadows or gradients. Muted, Kaleido-style e-ink color: slate teal (NT), faded brick (OT), dusty ochre (Psalms/Proverbs). A partner's marks render as a dithered halftone, yours as solid fills. Square tick boxes with heavy ink borders.
STORY: Open, see the date and three references large, read on paper, tick each. See your partner's halftone marks and the week's grid. Being behind is a quiet line, never red.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: app name and sync state, then date (largest text) with "Week 1, day 1". Three rows: tick box left, reference 26px, track name under it, a tiny link icon. Below: "This week", a 3×5 grid (tracks × Mon–Fri). Bottom: a standard four-tab bar.
FORM: The Plan Sheet (candidate 1 of 7, Impeccable's pick, chosen by Seth over the roll). seed:cb08ccb2e8a31892
SIGNATURE: Ticking a box plays a tiny e-ink partial refresh: the box inverts to black for about 120ms, then settles to its track color as the check draws itself. Under reduced motion it changes instantly.
