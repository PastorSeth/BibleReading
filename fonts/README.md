# Fonts

All self-hosted WOFF2 files (Latin subset) downloaded from Google Fonts on 2026-11-26. All are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (free to use, embed and share with the app): https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

- **Vollkorn Bold (700)** by Friedrich Althausen: the date, readings and headings. Standard (lining) numbers are switched on in style.css.
- **Alegreya Sans Regular (400) and Bold (700)** by Juan Pablo del Peral (Huerta Tipográfica): labels, buttons and all small text.

One more font, not from Google Fonts:

- **Biró Script** by Ingo Zimmermann (ingoFonts), `biro-script.otf`: the handwriting in the Gospel bridge drawing (Extras → The Gospel). Copied from the First Ten Days app, where it is used with permission.
